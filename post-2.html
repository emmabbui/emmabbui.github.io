COLOR PALETTES THAT ITCH MY BRAIN RIGHT

5 min

Learning more about colors and typography, I find it fun, especially in learning graphic design. Here are some fun colors that I think go well together:

#4E5166 , #7C90A0 , #B9B7A7 , #747274 #EEF0F2 , #A2999E , #846A6A , #353B3C #E4AEC5 , #5F7464 , #243D25 #594100 , #FFC6C6 , #FFDBDB

There's something so satisfying when a group of colors just clicks. I can't explain it, but it's a little like ASMR for my eyes. It's like a weird itch getting scratched. I find myself screenshotting color palettes when I see them in random places: on packaging, on social media, or even just nature. Sometimes I don’t even use the palettes for a specific design. I just like collecting them. But when I do start designing something, such as a poster, a website section, or even a logo,I’ll go back to these palettes and try them out. Some of them make everything look soft and calm, while others feel energetic and playful.

Learning how color communicates mood was a next step for me. I used to pick colors just because they looked pretty, but now I think about balance, accessibility, and contrast. The more I study color, the more I realize how subtle but powerful it is in design.
